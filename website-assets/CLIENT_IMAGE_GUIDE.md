## 🔄 How to Replace Any Image:
1. Open the relevant folder above.
2. Prepare your replacement photo in the recommended aspect ratio (`16:9` for Capabilities, `4:3` for Galleries).
3. Save the new image with the **exact same filename** in that folder.
4. The website will automatically update!
